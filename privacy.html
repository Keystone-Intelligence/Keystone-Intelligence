Privacy policy

What personal data we collect through this website and when you get in touch, why we use it, who else handles it, and your rights over it.

Last updated: 8 November 2026

1. Who we are

Keystone Intelligence Group is the trading name of Keystone Intelligence Strategy and Advisory Limited, a company registered in Ireland (company number 815739), with its registered office at 21 Clonard Road, Dundrum, Dublin. We are the data controller for the personal data described in this policy.

For any question about your data, email info@keystoneintelligencegroup.com.

This policy covers this website and anyone who contacts us or books a call. When we build a system for a client, the client's data is handled under our contract and data processing agreement with that client, not under this policy.

2. What we collect

When you book a call

Our "Book a call" buttons open a booking page run by Google Calendar. To book, you give your name and email address, and you can add a phone number and a note about what you'd like to discuss. We receive what you enter there.

When you email or phone us

We keep your name, contact details and whatever you choose to tell us in that conversation.

When you visit the website

Our hosting provider processes your IP address and basic details of each request (the page asked for, the time, your browser) so it can deliver the site and protect it from abuse.

If you agree to analytics cookies, Google Analytics also records how the site is used: which pages are viewed, how you arrived, the type of device and browser, and your approximate location (city or country). If you don't agree, Google Analytics is not loaded at all.

We don't ask for, and don't want, sensitive information such as health details through the website. Please don't include it in a booking note or a first email.

3. Why we use it, and our legal basis

  • To reply to you and arrange the call you asked for. Taking steps at your request before a possible contract, and our legitimate interest in answering enquiries.
  • To keep a record of enquiries and correspondence. Our legitimate interest in running the business, and our legal obligations to keep certain records.
  • To run the website and keep it secure. Our legitimate interest in a working, secure site.
  • To understand how the site is used, through Google Analytics. Your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

We don't sell personal data, and we don't use it for automated decisions about you.

4. Who else handles it

We use a small number of service providers, who handle data on our behalf and only on our instructions:

ProviderWhat for
Google Ireland Limited (Google Workspace)Our email, calendar, call booking page and documents
Google Ireland Limited (Google Analytics)Website usage statistics, only if you consent

We may also share data with our professional advisers, such as accountants and lawyers, where they need it to advise us, and where the law requires us to.

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Where they do, the transfer is covered by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified under it, or by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

5. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries and call bookings that don't lead to work: 12 months from our last contact, then deleted.
  • Clients: correspondence and records for as long as we work together, and then for six years, which is how long Irish law allows for claims and requires for tax records.
  • Website analytics: up to 14 months in Google Analytics, if you consented.
  • Hosting logs: kept by our hosting provider for a short period for security and troubleshooting.

6. How we protect it

Access to personal data is limited to the people at Keystone who need it for the purposes above. Our email, calendar and documents are held in Google Workspace and this website is hosted by Vercel. Both encrypt data while it's being sent and while it's stored. We review our own systems in line with ISO 27001 principles.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • correct data that's wrong or incomplete
  • delete your data, in certain circumstances
  • limit how we use it, in certain circumstances
  • stop using it where we rely on our legitimate interests
  • give you your data in a portable format

Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time. Withdrawing doesn't affect anything we did before you withdrew.

Email info@keystoneintelligencegroup.com to use any of these rights. We'll reply within one month. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Data Protection Commission at www.dataprotection.ie.

8. Cookies

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser.

Without your consent, we set no cookies. We remember whether you accepted or declined analytics in your browser's local storage, so we don't ask on every page.
