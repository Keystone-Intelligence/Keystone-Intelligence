The Partners

Operators first. Advisors second.

The partners behind Keystone are business owners. Plural. Across waste management, financial services, healthcare, construction and professional services. Every one of those businesses built from the ground up by people who were responsible for revenue, margin, operations, and people, simultaneously, with real consequences.

The AI work that came out of running those businesses was not an experiment. It was a necessity. When you have a 30-person operation losing hours every week to manual processes that should be automatic, you don't call a consultant. You fix it yourself.

That experience (knowing exactly where the pain is, what it costs, and what a working solution looks like) is what Keystone brings to every engagement. Not theory applied to your business. Pattern recognition from having lived it in ours.

Leadership

Jamie Walsh

As CFO of Kollect on Demand, he led the company's IPO on the Nasdaq First North exchange in Stockholm, ahead of its acquisition by an Irish private equity firm. It is exactly the kind of operational and financial pressure that Keystone's work is built to take off a founder's plate.

He leads the firm alongside its wider partner group.

Every one of those systems is live in a business a Keystone partner operates today. They handle real money, real customers, and real consequences. They work. The numbers prove it. That's the only reason we sell them.

And because the partners run the financial side of the business as well as the operational side, every workflow Keystone scopes has a payback case attached. If the numbers don't work, we don't build it. Cost clarity isn't a marketing position. It's the standard we hold ourselves to in our own businesses.
