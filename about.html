About Keystone

Operators first. Software second.

Keystone Intelligence Group is an Irish company with offices in Dublin, Waterford and Atlanta. We build AI systems for businesses, at a fixed price, and we speak business, not tech.

How Keystone started

We run businesses. We built AI into one of our own companies first, put it to work on the jobs nobody wanted, and costs came down by about a quarter.

We showed it to a room of business owners one Friday afternoon. By Monday they were asking us to build the same for their businesses.
