The Hub Model

Build once. Every system after costs less.

The first build is the expensive one, and it's expensive for a reason. It pays for the data layer, the integrations into the tools you already run, and the intelligence layer that everything else sits on top of. That foundation gets built once.

Every system after that plugs into what already exists. The second costs less than the first. The third costs less again. You're not paying to lay the plumbing each time — you're adding to a hub that's already standing.

It compounds hardest for owners running more than one company. Build the intelligence hub once, then deploy the same operation across every business you own. You pay for the foundation a single time.

Where the economics come from

  • The first build lays the foundation. Data layer, integrations and intelligence layer, built once.
  • Every next system plugs in. New builds reuse what already exists instead of starting over.
  • Costs fall as you go. The second system is cheaper than the first, the third cheaper again.
  • Multiple businesses, one hub. Owners pay for the core once and deploy it across all of them.

Questions

What business owners ask before they build.

What is custom AI development for a business?

It's building an AI system around how your business actually runs, instead of buying a generic tool and forcing your operation to fit it. We connect to your real data, automate the repetitive work that eats your team's week, and put the result into production. You own the system, and it runs on your own tools rather than a demo environment.

How long does it take to build a custom AI system?

Typically four weeks from kickoff to a system live on your data. The first build takes the longest because it lays the foundation — the data layer, the integrations, and the intelligence layer. Every system we add after that is faster, because the hard part already exists.

What does custom AI development cost for an Irish SME?

The build is a fixed price between €1,000 and €20,000, agreed in writing before any work begins. Before that, a €1,000 Discovery Report maps and costs exactly what to build, and it's credited against the build if you go ahead. Adding a further system to the hub starts from €1,000. See full pricing for the detail.

Can you connect to the systems I already use like Xero, Salesforce, or my CRM?

Yes, and that's the whole point of custom development. We build on top of the tools you already run — Xero, Salesforce, your CRM, your job management software — so the system reads and writes where your data already lives. No rip-and-replace. What happens after the first system is built?

You expand. New agents and systems bolt onto the hub you already own, and each one costs less than the last because the foundation is already paid for. Some clients run several businesses on the same hub — they pay for the core once and deploy it across all of them.

Is the price actually fixed or does it change during the build?

It's fixed. Scope, price and delivery date are agreed in writing before we start, and there are no change orders inside the agreed scope. If the build takes longer than planned, that's on us, not on your invoice. We also agree the outcome metric up front, so you know what the system has to deliver.
