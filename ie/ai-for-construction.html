Cost · Lost work

Tenders submitted and forgotten

The pipeline spreadsheet has the bid date but not the chase date. Three weeks after submission nobody has followed up, and the work has gone elsewhere.

Cost · Missed windows

Planning and funding status nobody tracks

A Sport Ireland grant outcome that triggered a four-week tender window sat in someone's inbox. A planning permission came through unannounced. Status changes that should trigger action go unnoticed.

Cost · Wasted capacity

QS capacity in the wrong place

Two senior QSs on the same project. A junior idle while a senior works overtime. Nobody sees the global capacity picture, so allocation happens by memory.

Cost · Cash held up

Contract data retyped into accounts

Payment milestones, retention percentages and variation orders sit in the signed contract, then get re-entered line by line into another system. Errors creep in. Milestones get missed. Cash sits longer than it should.

Cost · Absorbed disputes

No audit trail when it goes wrong

When there's a question about what was agreed, the team scrambles through emails to reconstruct it. Often it can't be reconstructed. Often the firm absorbs the cost.

What Keystone Built

The construction hub.
Built for a specialist contractor.

This isn't what AI could do for construction. It's what we've built. We built a specialist contractor working across Ireland and the UK one platform for its full commercial lifecycle, replacing a patchwork of spreadsheets and shared folders where tender deadlines, contracts and payment schedules each lived in a different file.

Every opportunity runs through an eighteen-stage tender pipeline from first enquiry to handover, in euro and sterling, with QS assignments, deadlines, funding status and planning status visible at a glance. Upload a signed contract PDF and the model extracts milestone names, amounts, percentages, trigger conditions and due dates, turning them into a live payment schedule in under 30 seconds.

From there, milestone-driven invoicing generates draft invoices one milestone at a time or in bulk, tracked from draft to issued to paid. And every won deal gets its own project delivery workspace: document checklist, financial dashboard tracking budget against actual, variation orders, subcontractor roster with PO matching, and weekly progress reporting.

The newest part reads tenders. A tender pack can run to dozens of documents and scanned drawings. The hub reads the whole pack and produces a Word summary and a draft bill of quantities for the estimators. It never submits anything, and the contractor's own rates are never sent to the AI.

Common Questions

What construction MDs ask first.

How can AI help an Irish construction company win more tenders?

AI helps Irish construction companies win more tenders by giving the commercial team full visibility on the pipeline at every stage. An eighteen-stage tender pipeline tracks every opportunity from first enquiry to handover, surfaces tenders that need chasing before they go cold, and ensures QS capacity is allocated to the highest-value opportunities rather than managed by memory. The firms winning more tenders are not necessarily cheaper, they are better organised.

What does AI actually do for construction pipeline management?

AI-powered pipeline management tracks every opportunity through defined stages, assigns owners to each next action, monitors planning and funding status automatically, and surfaces tenders that need follow-up before they go cold. Keystone Intelligence has built exactly this system for a specialist contractor working across Ireland and the UK.

Can AI extract payment schedules from contract PDFs automatically?

Yes. Keystone Intelligence has built an AI system that extracts milestone names, amounts, percentages, trigger conditions and due dates from a signed contract PDF and turns them into a live payment schedule in under 30 seconds. The work that previously required a junior to re-type line items into the accounts package now happens automatically, with the commercial team reviewing the output rather than building it from scratch.
