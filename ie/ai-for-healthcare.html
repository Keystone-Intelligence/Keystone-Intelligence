Where the Time Goes

Five categories eat the day.
None of them need a clinician to run.

For most practices, the admin falls into the same five places. Each one needs organised systems, reliable information and consistent follow-through — not clinical expertise.

01 · Notes

Clinical dictation and note-writing

Consultation notes, letters to consultants, referral letters. Typically 60–90 seconds per consultation for dictation alone, with transcription time on top.

02 · Referrals

Referral management

Sending referrals, tracking whether they've been received, chasing responses, and filing letters when they finally arrive.

03 · Recall

Patient recall and chronic disease management

Finding patients due for annual reviews, diabetes checks, smear tests and flu vaccines — then calling or messaging each one to book them in.

04 · Prescriptions

Repeat prescription processing

Reviewing requests, checking appropriateness, issuing and filing. High volume, low complexity, but it still takes process and time.

05 · Communications

General patient communications

Answering queries, confirming appointments, sending results with the right information, and following up on missed appointments.

The pattern

Admin, not medicine

Every one of these sits on a to-do list that a system can work through automatically, accurately, and without needing clinical attention.

GDPR and Data Protection

Patient data is special category data.
We build like it.

Healthcare data is among the most sensitive personal data there is. Any AI used in an Irish practice has to comply with GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018 — and because health data is special category data under Article 9, the bar is higher than in most sectors.

Keystone designs every healthcare system with data protection built in from the ground up, not added as an afterthought. Hosting, retention and access are decided in the first week of the build, because they are almost impossible to reverse cheaply afterwards.

Processing patient data for clinical and administrative purposes falls under Article 9(2)(h). Your Data Processing Agreement with Keystone documents this clearly for your DPIA.

What every healthcare build includes

  • EU-hosted infrastructure, exclusively. Patient data stays within the EU and EEA at all times.
  • Data minimisation by design. The system processes only what it needs. Dictation tools transcribe; they don't retain audio recordings.
  • Role-based access control. Receptionists, nurses and GPs each see only the patient data relevant to their role.
  • Full audit logs. Every data access event is logged, so "who saw this record" is a query, not an investigation.
  • A Data Processing Agreement with every engagement. What data is processed, for what purpose, on what legal basis, and for how long.

Common Questions

What practices ask before they book.

How can AI reduce admin time in an Irish GP practice?

AI reduces admin time in an Irish GP practice by automating the tasks that surround the consultation rather than replacing clinical judgement: dictation and note-writing, referral tracking, patient recall for chronic disease management, and repeat prescription processing. Irish GP practices typically lose three to five hours a day to this kind of administrative overhead. Keystone Intelligence identifies the highest-value process to automate first on a free discovery call.

Does Keystone replace my existing practice management system like Socrates?

No. Keystone Intelligence builds AI systems that connect to your existing practice management software rather than replacing it. If you use Socrates, Health One, or a similar system, the AI layer integrates directly with it so notes, referrals, and recalls appear in the right place without a second system to log into or manage.
