The Fee-Earner Time Problem

Time is the product.
Admin is the leak.

In a professional services firm the most expensive resource is the qualified person. The solicitor, the accountant, the consultant, the architect. Their time is what you sell. Yet a large slice of every week goes on work that never touches a client bill.

The hidden cost

Every admin hour is unbilled

Notes written up after a call. Timesheets reconstructed from memory. Invoices chased. None of it goes on a client bill, and all of it comes off your most valuable people's day.

The real bottleneck

The systems, not the talent

Your people are good at what they do. The problem isn't talent — it's the systems around the talent. Manual steps, re-keyed data and inboxes that never empty.

The fix

Remove the admin, keep the person

AI doesn't replace the qualified judgement clients pay for. It clears the routine work around it, so fee-earners spend their day on the part only they can do.

Questions, Answered

What firms ask before they book.

How can AI give fee-earners more billable time?

In most firms, qualified people lose hours every day to admin — writing up notes, chasing billing, sorting the inbox. AI takes that work off their desk. The fee-earner reviews and approves instead of typing from scratch. That time goes back into billable work, often around eight hours a week per person.

Can AI automate client call notes for a solicitor practice?

Yes. Every client call is recorded, transcribed and turned into a structured action list. What used to take 25 minutes of post-call write-up now takes about three, because the solicitor edits a draft instead of starting from a blank page. We go into more detail in our guide to AI for Irish solicitors.

What does AI automation cost for a professional services firm in Ireland?

It starts with a free 20-minute Discovery Call. If it's worth going further, a Discovery Report is €1,000, delivered in 48 hours and credited against the build. Builds are fixed price, €1,000–€20,000, usually live in about four weeks. New tools added later start from €1,000 each. The price is agreed in writing before any work begins.

How does AI connect to practice management systems like Keyhouse or Actionstep?

We build around the systems you already run. The work sits on top of your practice management software — Keyhouse, Actionstep or whatever you use — so notes, actions and billing land in the right place. No rip-and-replace.

Which professional services firms benefit most from AI?

Any firm where the main cost is qualified people doing repetitive admin: solicitor practices, accountancy firms, management consultancies, architectural practices and financial advisory firms. If your best people spend hours on work a system could handle, there's time to win back. See the live systems on our case studies page.
