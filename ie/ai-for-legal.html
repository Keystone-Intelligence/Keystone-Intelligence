Questions, Answered

What solicitors ask before they book.

How can AI reduce admin time for an Irish solicitor practice?

Most of the admin in a busy practice is rule-based: picking the right claim form for the hearing, assigning fee codes, calculating mileage at approved rates, tracking payments and writing up client calls. AI handles that work from data that already exists in your court diary and case files, and the solicitor reviews and signs off. In a three fee-earner practice that is typically around twenty hours of admin a month recovered, and post-call write-ups drop from twenty-five minutes to three.

Do I need to switch practice management systems to use Keystone's AI?

No. We build on top of the practice management system you already run — Keyhouse, Actionstep or whatever your firm uses. You keep your system, your data and the workflows your staff already know. The automation sits above it and targets the specific bottleneck costing you the most time.

Can AI automate Legal Aid claim forms like LA1 and LA10?

Yes. When a hearing outcome is logged, the system knows which form is required — LA1, LA10, CLA11 or CF1 — and generates it from existing case data, with the correct fee codes and mileage already filled in. The solicitor reviews and signs off, and the submission is logged for payment tracking. Forms that take 25–35 minutes to complete by hand are generated in under two minutes. We go into more detail in our guide to AI for Irish solicitors.

Is Keystone's AI compliant with solicitor confidentiality and data obligations?

Compliance comes down to how a system is deployed, so we settle it in the first week of a build rather than after go-live. Privileged communications are not special category data under GDPR, but they carry professional obligations that overlap heavily with it. Every Keystone build runs on EU-hosted infrastructure, and we provide a written Data Processing Agreement with every engagement. Systems handling case data need a clear retention policy and an audit trail you can actually produce — in our call-actions build, every action carries a transcript reference. As the practice deploying the system, you remain the data controller. We cover the detail in our guide to AI and GDPR in Ireland.

What does an AI system cost for an Irish legal practice?

It starts with a free 20-minute Discovery Call. If it's worth going further, a Discovery Report is €1,000, delivered in 48 hours and credited against the build. Builds are fixed price, €1,000–€20,000, agreed in writing before any work begins. New tools added later start from €1,000 each. There is no licence fee and no retainer — hosting and model processing are charged on usage, typically a small fraction of the build cost.

How long before an AI system is live in my practice?

Typically about four weeks from kickoff to live. Because we build on the system you already run, there is no platform migration to plan around first. You start with the automation that addresses your biggest time loss, then add more once you have seen the return.
