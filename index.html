Trust and security

Built to pass your IT team's review.

Checked before it goes live

Every system goes through Keystone Sentry, our security review: 67 controls across eight areas, designed in line with ISO 27001 principles and covering the risks specific to AI. You get the written report.

EU GDPR

We're an Irish company working under EU GDPR. Any work on your data outside the EU is covered by Standard Contractual Clauses and a transfer impact assessment.

US HIPAA

For US healthcare clients we build to the HIPAA Security Rule and sign a Business Associate Agreement before we touch patient data.

Only the access it needs

Each system asks for the narrowest permissions that do the job, and your IT team sees them all before anything is switched on.

Not used for training

We use business-grade AI services on terms that don't allow your data to be used to train their models.

Future-proof by design

We're not tied to any one AI provider. As better models arrive, your system can move to them without starting again.
