Questions

What business owners ask us first.

What does an AI consultancy do?

A good one builds working AI systems, not slide decks. We don't hand you a strategy document and leave. We connect to your real data, automate the repetitive work that eats your team's week, and put the result into production on the tools you already run. You end up owning a system that does the job — not a plan for one. Our how it works page walks through the whole process.

How much does AI consultancy cost in Ireland?

It starts with a free 20-minute Discovery Call. If it's worth going further, a €1,000 Discovery Report maps and costs exactly what to build — delivered within 48 hours and credited against the build if you proceed. The build itself is a fixed price between €1,000 and €20,000, agreed in writing before any work begins. No retainers, no hourly billing, no surprise invoices. See full pricing for the detail.

How long does it take to build a custom AI system for my business?

About four weeks from kickoff to a system live on your own data. The first build takes the longest because it lays the foundation — the data layer, the integrations, and the intelligence layer. Everything you add after that is faster, because the hard part is already built.

Is AI suitable for Irish SMEs turning over less than €50 million?

Yes — that's exactly who we build for. Most of our work is with owner-run Irish businesses well under €50 million in turnover, where a few automated systems free up real hours and real margin. You don't need a big data team or a big budget. If you have revenue, costs, and operations, the patterns apply.

What is business process automation?

It's using software to run the repetitive, rules-based work your team does by hand — moving data between systems, chasing invoices, updating records, generating documents. Instead of a person doing the same task hundreds of times a month, a system does it in the background. That's most of what we build: the admin that quietly eats your week, handed to software that doesn't miss and doesn't tire. The AI glossary explains the rest of the terms in plain English.
