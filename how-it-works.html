Step 01 · Free
01

The Discovery Call

Twenty minutes. No cost. No obligation. We use it to understand the business, the pressure points, and where AI might genuinely move a metric. By the end of the call, you know whether Keystone is the right fit. If we're not, we'll tell you that — and we'll tell you why.

This is not a sales call dressed up as a consultation. It is a diagnostic. We ask specific questions about your workflows, your revenue model, your team, and your biggest operational pain points. The output is an honest read on where AI can and cannot add value for your specific business.

What we cover Your top operational pain points. Where revenue is slipping. What your team spends time on that a system should handle.
What you leave with A clear, honest read on whether your business is ready for AI, and where we'd start if it is.

03

Build the Hub

We build the core intelligence hub for your business. Fixed scope, fixed price, typically four weeks. The same people who scoped the report are the ones building it — no handoffs to a delivery team, no account managers playing telephone. The hub goes live on your real data with your team using it from day one.

The hub becomes the foundation everything else gets built on. It connects to your existing systems, holds your data, and runs the first set of agents we identified together. Whatever scale you start at, you own the whole thing.

One core hub A single intelligence layer connecting your existing systems. Reusable across multiple workflows and, in time, multiple businesses.
The first agents go live The highest-priority workflows from the Discovery Report, built into the hub and running on day one.
You own it The hub belongs to you. No vendor lock-in. No platform fees to Keystone. The code, the data, the systems — yours.
Fixed price, no surprises Scope, price, and delivery date agreed in writing before any build starts. We build exactly what we scoped.
Step 04 · Ongoing
04

Expand the Hub

Once the hub is running, new agents and workflows go on top of it. And because the hard part — the data, the connections, the intelligence layer — already exists, each new app is faster and cheaper than the last. The more you run the hub, the more it compounds.

The hub also extends across multiple businesses. If you run several companies, the same core layer can serve all of them. One hub. Many businesses. Each new application using the data and patterns the others have already established.

Compounding economies of scale Every new app benefits from the data and infrastructure the hub already has. Build cost drops with every iteration.
One hub, many businesses If you run more than one company, the hub can serve all of them. The intelligence travels with you.
Quarterly priorities We work with you to identify the highest-value next app, build it on the existing hub, and measure the result.
Stop whenever you want No retainers, no lock-in. The hub keeps running whether we're building on it or not. You decide when there's another app worth commissioning.
