Questions

What people usually ask

What does it cost?

The first call is free. A discovery document is €1,000, delivered in 48 hours and credited against the build if you go ahead. Builds are fixed price, typically €1,000 to €20,000, with half paid up front. We never charge per seat or per user.

How long does it take?

Most builds are live in about four weeks. The discovery document sets the dates before any work starts.

Do we have to replace our current systems?

No. If a system you already use does the job, we connect to it. We only rebuild something when it's much cheaper or more reliable to do so.

Is our data safe?

Every system goes through our security review before it goes live, and you get the report. We work under EU GDPR, and we can sign a Business Associate Agreement for US healthcare work. See the security section on our homepage for the detail.

Do you work outside Ireland?

Yes. We have clients in Ireland, the UK and the United States, and an office in Atlanta.

Which AI models do you use?

Business-grade models on terms that don't allow your data to be used for training. We aren't tied to one provider, so your system can move to better models as they arrive.
