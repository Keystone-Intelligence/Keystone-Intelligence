How fixed-price works

Every number on this page is agreed in writing before any work begins. Scope, price, and delivery date are signed off together. If the workflow we build doesn't deliver what we said it would, we fix it. Common Questions

What you'll probably want to ask.

How does fixed-price AI consultancy work?

The price for each step is agreed in writing before work begins, alongside the scope and delivery date. We don't issue change orders against work that's inside the agreed scope. If something takes us longer than we expected, that's on us.

How much does it cost to build an AI workflow for my business?

Between €1,000 and €20,000, fixed, depending on what the workflow has to do. An email-triage agent for a founder inbox sits at the lower end. A multi-agent operations hub with deep integrations into Salesforce and Xero sits at the higher end. The Discovery Report tells you exactly where your build lands inside that range before you commit. For the payback maths behind three live builds, see what AI actually costs an Irish business.

What is included in an AI discovery report?

A free 20-minute Discovery Call, then a 1–2 hour deep-dive call with you and the people closest to the workflows in question, then a written report within 48 hours of that call. The report covers the top three AI opportunities for your business, what each would cost to build, the projected return, and the architecture sketch behind it. €1,000 credited in full against Workflow Development if you commission Step 03.

Does AI development get cheaper over time?

Yes, substantially. The hard part is done once. The first build pays for the data layer, the integrations, the intelligence layer, and the governance around them. Every additional workflow plugs into that platform, so it costs less and ships faster than the one before it. The compounding economics matter most to operators running multiple businesses — one platform, many use cases.

Do I need to sign a retainer for AI consultancy?

No. The fixed-price builds are exactly that — fixed. You commission a workflow, we build it, you pay the agreed price. No standing monthly fees, no minimum spend.

Can I get the AI report without committing to a full build?

Yes. The Discovery Report is decision-grade material on its own. You can take it away, build the workflows in-house, hand the report to another team, or simply use it to decide AI isn't the right move right now. The €1,000 is only credited if you proceed to a build with us.
